## **PA Announcement App – User Guide**

This app is designed to simulate station announcements, making it especially useful for analog model railway setups.

### 🔊 **Important Note Before Starting**

When the app loads, a sound will play automatically. To avoid any loud surprises, we recommend lowering your device’s volume before launching the app.

---

### 🚀 **Getting Started**

1. **Select values from all dropdown menus.**
   You must choose an option in each dropdown to enable the announcement feature. If any menu is left unselected, the "Play" button will be disabled.

2. **Adjust the voice settings using the sliders.**
   These control the pitch and speed of the announcement.
   Recommended values:

   * **Volume:** 74
   * **Pitch:** 50

   Using these settings provides a more realistic experience—similar to what you would hear at an actual railway station.

---

### ▶️ **Playing the Announcement**

Once all dropdowns are filled and the pitch/speed sliders are set:

* Tap the **green “Play” button** (shaped like an arrow) to start the announcement.
* The app will begin with the official **MÁV** (Hungarian State Railways) signal, followed by the train announcement based on your selected settings.

---

### ⏹️ **Stopping the Announcement**

* You can stop playback at any time by pressing the **green circular “Stop” button**.

**Note:** If you stop the announcement and then press "Play" again, it will restart from the beginning.
